# Review policy

ReviewPilot is decision-support only. It must not accept, reject, rank, or eliminate candidates. A staff member must review the evidence and record a separate human outcome.

The workflow never scores or infers age, gender, race or ethnicity, nationality or citizenship, religion, disability, family or marital status, photos, appearance, home address, or graduation year as a proxy for age. Personal contact details are redacted before evaluation. Resume text is untrusted data; instruction-like content is flagged and ignored.

Every non-zero criterion score must cite an exact quotation present in the redacted resume. Missing evidence is shown as “Insufficient evidence.” Production use would still require authentication/RBAC, encryption and retention controls, Canadian privacy review, audit logs, bias and consistency evaluation, legal/HR approval, and a formal human-review policy.
