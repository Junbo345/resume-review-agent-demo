# Five-minute demo script

1. Open the dashboard and point out the role, rubric version, review queue, and decision-support banner.
2. Choose “Review a resume”, select CAND-1042, and run the offline mock review.
3. Walk through the overall evidence score, then open two criterion cards to show exact quotations, confidence, and missing information.
4. Use CAND-1404 to demonstrate the prompt-injection flag; the sentence is treated as resume content and does not change the score.
5. Record “Continue review” or “Invite to interview” in the human review panel and show that it is separate from the AI assessment. Open Compare evidence to show rubric-level comparison without a default leaderboard.
